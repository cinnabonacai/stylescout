# Fall/Winter 2026 Trend Notes

Curated style notes used as the RAG corpus's unstructured half (the catalog CSV is the structured half). Each entry is treated as a retrievable document, tagged with the aesthetic/style keywords it maps to.

---

## trend_001: Quiet Luxury Continues
tags: minimalist, tailored, classic
Quiet luxury remains dominant this season: oversized tailored blazers, wide-leg trousers, and cream/taupe neutral palettes. Investment pieces over logos. Pairs well with structured top-handle bags and Chelsea boots.

## trend_002: Utilitarian Layering
tags: utilitarian, streetwear, layering
Cargo pants, quilted vests, and longline puffers are back, usually layered over a simple base layer (turtleneck or hoodie). Olive, navy, and charcoal dominate. Combat boots or chunky sneakers finish the look.

## trend_003: Dark Academia Preppy Revival
tags: preppy, cozy, classic
Houndstooth, cable-knit cardigans, pleated midi skirts, and knit berets. Camel, burgundy, and cream color story. Leans elevated-casual, good for office or campus settings.

## trend_004: Going-Out Edge
tags: edgy, going-out, statement
Leather mini skirts, mesh tops, and cropped leather jackets for night-out looks. Gold chain layering necklaces or statement earrings as the finishing touch. Combat or platform boots over heels this season.

## trend_005: Elevated Basics
tags: elevated, minimalist, versatile
Silk button-downs and white leather sneakers are the season's "elevated basic" pairing — dressy enough for the office, casual enough for the weekend. Works across almost every other trend as a neutral anchor piece.

## trend_006: Budget-Conscious Capsule Dressing
tags: versatile, classic, casual
The emphasis this season for budget-conscious dressers is a small capsule of versatile pieces that recombine: one neutral coat, one pair of dark denim, one classic boot, and 2-3 tops that mix across tailored and casual contexts.
